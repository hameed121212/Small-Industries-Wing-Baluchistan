How Multi-Device Sync Works for Staff & Administrators

1. Entry from Any Mobile / PC

When a trainee applies online, or when staff adds an entry from a mobile phone or laptop, it immediately updates the cloud master database.

2. Admin Desk Anywhere

When logged in on any computer or phone, the portal automatically checks for updates every 12 seconds and whenever you switch back to the browser tab.

3. Universal Passphrase

Changing the administrator passphrase on one device instantly updates the cloud. All other devices will immediately require and accept the new passphrase.
